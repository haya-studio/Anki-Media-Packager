Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. Are my files uploaded to any server?
A. No. Your files are processed locally in your browser and are not uploaded to our server.
Q. What if I have multiple files with the same name?
A. The tool will flag them as 'Duplicate' and prevent generation to avoid linking errors. Please rename duplicates to ensure 1-to-1 matching.
